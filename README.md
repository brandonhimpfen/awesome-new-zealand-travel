New Zealand is a breathtaking South Pacific destination featuring dramatic alpine ranges, deep fiords, volcanic geothermal regions, and pristine national parks. This list is intended for outdoor adventurers, alpine hikers, road-trip travelers, and nature conservationists.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, Electronic Travel Authority applications, tourism boards, and weather tracking.

- [100% Pure New Zealand (Official Tourism Portal)](https://www.newzealand.com/) - Official national travel guide featuring destination highlights, interactive itinerary planners, and regional guides.
- [New Zealand Electronic Travel Authority (NZeTA)](https://nzeta.immigration.govt.nz/) - Official Immigration New Zealand portal for applying for visitor entry authorizations and paying the International Visitor Conservation and Tourism Levy (IVL).
- [Department of Conservation (DOC)](https://www.doc.govt.nz/) - Official government agency managing national parks, Great Walks booking systems, backcountry huts, and conservation tracks.
- [MetService New Zealand](https://www.metservice.com/) - Official national meteorological service providing regional weather forecasts, severe weather warnings, and alpine snow reports.

## Transportation

Navigating inter-island ferries, domestic airlines, scenic train journeys, and public transit.

- [Interislander Ferry Service](https://www.interislander.co.nz/) - Primary passenger and vehicle ferry linking Wellington (North Island) and Picton (South Island) across the Cook Strait.
- [Bluebridge Cook Strait Ferries](https://www.bluebridge.co.nz/) - Inter-island ferry service providing passenger and car transport between the North and South Islands.
- [Air New Zealand](https://www.airnewzealand.co.nz/) - National flag carrier operating extensive domestic flight networks across regional airports and international hubs.
- [Great Journeys New Zealand](https://www.greatjourneysnz.com/) - Scenic rail operator running iconic passenger train routes including the TranzAlpine, Northern Explorer, and Coastal Pacific.

## Destinations & Regions

### Auckland & North Island North

- [Auckland Tourism (AucklandNZ)](https://www.aucklandnz.com/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the City of Sails, harbor islands (Rangitoto, Waiheke), and black-sand surf beaches.
- [Northland & Bay of Islands Guide](https://www.newzealand.com/) - Regional portal for exploring historic Waitangi Treaty Grounds, subtropical kauri forests, and marine reserves.

### Wellington & Central North Island

- [WellingtonNZ (Official City Portal)](https://www.wellingtonnz.com/) - Official visitor guide for the capital city's craft coffee culture, Te Papa museum, and cable car.
- [Tongariro Alpine Crossing Official Portal](https://www.doc.govt.nz/) - Official Department of Conservation safety guide and track planner for New Zealand's premier one-day alpine hike.
- [RotoruaNZ Tourism Portal](https://www.rotoruanz.com/) - Visitor hub for exploring geothermal valleys, Māori cultural experiences, and Redwood forest mountain biking.

### Christchurch & Canterbury

- [ChristchurchNZ Tourism Guide](https://www.christchurchnz.com/) - Official portal for exploring the garden city, transitional architecture, and Port Hills coastal tracks.
- [Tekapo Tourism & Dark Sky Reserve](https://www.newzealand.com/) - Visitor guide for Lake Tekapo, the Church of the Good Shepherd, and Mount John astronomical stargazing.

### Queenstown & Southern Lakes

- [Queenstown Official Visitor Guide](https://www.queenstownnz.co.nz/) - Regional tourism hub for adventure sports, alpine lake cruises, and surrounding Otago vineyards.
- [Wanaka Tourism (Lake Wanaka)](https://www.lakewanaka.co.nz/) - Official guide for Roy's Peak hiking trails, ski fields, and relaxed lakeside living.

### Fiordland & West Coast

- [Milford Sound / Piopiotahi Tourism Guide](https://www.newzealand.com/) - Official visitor portal for exploring Fiordland National Park fiords, boat cruises, and rainforest waterfalls.
- [Glacier Country Tourism (Franz Josef & Fox)](https://www.newzealand.com/) - Regional guide for exploring West Coast temperate rainforests and glacier valley walks.

## Safety & Biosecurity Regulations

Practical advice on biosecurity declarations, outdoor preparedness, and emergency services.

- [MPI Biosecurity New Zealand (Check It's Alright)](https://www.biosecurity.govt.nz/) - Official government guidelines on strict border declarations for food, plant material, and outdoor gear.
- [Mountain Safety Council (Outdoors-Advice)](https://www.mountainsafety.org.nz/) - National safety organization providing outdoor planning tools, avalanche advisories, and the 5-point Land Safety Code.
- [Emergency Services (Police, Fire, Ambulance - Dial 111)](https://www.police.govt.nz/) - Direct nationwide emergency dispatch number for immediate life-safety assistance.
